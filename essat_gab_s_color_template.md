# ESSAT Gabès Color Template

This color template is extracted from the ESSAT Gabès visual identity, focusing on a professional and academic aesthetic.

## Primary Colors
- **Primary Blue:** `#1E73BE` - Used for headers, primary buttons, and key branding elements.
- **Surface White:** `#FCF9F8` - The main background color for a clean, readable interface.

## Secondary & Accent Colors
- **Surface Dim:** `#DCD9D9` - Used for subtle dividers and secondary backgrounds.
- **Surface Container Low:** `#F6F3F2` - Ideal for cards and section backgrounds.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Lexend
- **Style:** Clean, modern, and highly legible for educational content.

## Design Tokens
- **Roundness:** 4px (Soft edges for a professional look)
- **Color Mode:** Light
